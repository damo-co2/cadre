What you get

Not a chatbot. A team.

Every Bot has a job, a memory and a computer of its own. You talk to one, and the work gets done by the right one.

Bots with a job

Named teammates with their own chat and memory. Start from 24 templates, such as Inbox Concierge, Market Scout or Repo Sentinel, or describe a role of your own.

A real computer each

Each Bot gets its own browser, shell and files. Watch any screen live, or take control when you need to.

A Chief of Staff

One Bot runs the rest. It hires specialists, hands out tasks, follows up and briefs you every morning.

Bring any model

Use the Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini subscription you already have, or any API key. Ollama works for fully local models.

Routines that run themselves

Schedules, webhooks or one click. Morning briefs, inbox sweeps, price checks and uptime watches, each with run history and limits.

You stay in charge

Bots change files only inside one folder. Anything risky, like paying, sending or pushing code, asks you first. Passwords live in an encrypted Vault.

How it works

Three steps to a working team.

No setup scripts, no prompt engineering. Tell it the job the way you'd tell a person.

Hire a Chief of Staff

Pick a model and start Cadre. Your first Bot introduces itself and asks a few questions about you and your work.

Tell it the job

"Keep my inbox at zero." "Watch our releases." The Chief hires the right specialists and splits the work up.

Watch Team pulse

One panel shows what every Bot is doing: live screens, the task board, open pull requests and what runs next.

Your model, your choice

Works with what you already pay for.

Set a default model, then give any Bot its own. Subscriptions are picked up from apps you're already signed in to.

Claude subscription ChatGPT subscription Gemini Anthropic API OpenAI API Google API xAI API OpenRouter Ollama (local)
Connects to your tools

Plugged into your day.

  • Apps: GitHub, Linear, Notion, Sentry, Slack, Gmail and Calendar, plus any MCP server.
  • Group chats: put 2 to 6 Bots in one room and @mention who should answer.
  • Teach a task: do it once on a Bot's screen and it turns that into a reusable skill.
  • On your phone: scan a QR code and chat with your team over Wi-Fi.
